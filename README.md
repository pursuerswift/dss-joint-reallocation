The combined archives include the original experiments, the endogenous-stopping comparison, complete 18 November sensitivity outputs, and the final focused experiments: 85 paired timing instances, moderate-cost compatibility at 100 repetitions, and six 100-repetition precision extensions. Costs and initial allocations are specified decision scenarios. Simulation value and historical implementation evidence are reported separately. Both improvements and conditions where simpler methods suffice are retained.

The original [17 November archive](DSS_v3_reproduction.zip), [instructions](REPRODUCE.md) and [file index](FILE_INDEX.csv) remain available for provenance. Raw historical cohort data are not redistributed. The current archive supplies code, configurations, complete simulation results and aggregate historical outputs.
